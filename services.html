Services

Building Dreams with Expertise You Can Trust

At Treeland, our services cover every stage of the real estate journey — from land acquisition and planning to design, construction, and delivery. We don’t just sell plots; we build entire lifestyles tailored to your needs.

🏡 1. Town Planning & Development

We specialize in developing gated communities and housing societies that combine modern design with functional urban planning. Every Treeland town is equipped with:

  • Smart road networks and underground electricity
  • Reliable water, sewerage, and gas systems
  • Green parks, playgrounds, and walking tracks
  • Mosques, schools, and healthcare facilities
  • Well-designed commercial zones for shopping and business

Our developments emphasize safety, sustainability, and convenience, making Treeland one of Pakistan’s most trusted names in housing.

🧱 2. Construction & Architectural Design

Treeland’s in-house planning department, led by expert architects and engineers, ensures every project meets the highest global standards. From modern apartments to luxury villas, we offer:

  • Customized design solutions for clients and investors
  • Smart energy-efficient layouts and ventilation
  • Earthquake-resistant structures and imported materials
  • Interior design consultancy for a complete living experience

We believe construction is an art — and every Treeland project is a masterpiece.

🏗️ 3. Real Estate Development & Investment Solutions

Whether you’re a landowner, buyer, or investor, Treeland provides joint-venture and per-kanal development systems for transparent and profitable partnerships. Our expert financial team assists in:

  • Feasibility & project evaluation
  • Design, financing, and sales strategy
  • Marketing and brand positioning of new societies
  • Corporate and individual investment opportunities

We turn your land into a thriving community — while maximizing value for every stakeholder.

🚧 4. Infrastructure & Civil Engineering

With decades of experience in motorways, pipelines, bridges, airports, and city infrastructure, Treeland ensures its housing projects stand on the strongest foundations. We deliver:

  • Sewerage and drainage systems
  • Water reservoirs and filtration plants
  • Roads and street networks
  • Power distribution and electrification
  • Landscaping, horticulture, and parking solutions

Our infrastructure work isn’t just technical — it’s transformative, designed to elevate lifestyles and improve everyday convenience.

💰 5. Financing & Property Management

We partner with major banks and financial institutions to offer flexible payment plans and secure mortgage options for our clients. Treeland’s dedicated after-sales and property management team ensures:

  • Hassle-free ownership transfer
  • Rental and resale assistance
  • Maintenance and community support services

Owning a home has never been this simple, transparent, and rewarding.
